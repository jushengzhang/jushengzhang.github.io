Hi, I'm Jusheng Zhang!

I am a Visiting Researcher at Stanford University, working with Joseph C. Wu and Xuekun Wu on agentic AI for life sciences, and a student researcher at Google DeepMind, working with Ming-Hsuan Yang and Liangzhe Yuan on efficient video and vision-language models. Previously, I was a research intern at Nanyang Technological University (with Kwok-Yan Lam and Yongsen Zheng), Meta AI (Llama 4 team), and Snap Research (with Jian Wang), and a researcher at the HCP Lab of Sun Yat-sen University (with Keze Wang and Liang Lin).

My goal is to help build the next generation of AI agents for scientific discovery.

Research

My research focuses on reliable AI agents and multimodal learning. My current interests include:

  • Agents & Reasoning: Multi-agent coordination, uncertainty-aware reasoning, and learning from failures. [Markets of Minds] [CE-Graph] [KABB] [GAM-Agent]
  • VLM: Efficient vision-language models, counterfactual adaptation, and video understanding. [HybridToken-VLM] [CF-VLM] [Video Encoders]
  • Robotics: Vision-language-action models and predictive world models for robotic manipulation. [E0] [E0.5] [ActPhysCause]
  • AI for Life Sciences: AI agents for biological research and scientific discovery.

  • CF-VLM: CounterFactual Vision-Language Fine-tuning

    Jusheng Zhang, Kaitong Cai, Yijia Fan, Jian Wang, Keze Wang

    NeurIPS 2025

    [Paper] [arXiv] [Code]

    Fine-tunes vision-language models with counterfactual image-text pairs so they stay aligned, distinguish factual from counterfactual scenes, and respond to minimal causal edits, improving compositional reasoning.

  • KABB: Knowledge-Aware Bayesian Bandits for Dynamic Expert Coordination

    Jusheng Zhang, Zimeng Huang, Yijia Fan, Ningyuan Liu, Mingyan Li, Zhuojie Yang, Jiawei Yao, Jian Wang, Keze Wang

    ICML 2025

    [Paper] [arXiv] [Code]

    KABB selects which expert agents to use for a task via Thompson Sampling guided by a semantic knowledge-distance model, with dual adaptation that keeps expert representations current.

  • GAM-Agent: Game-Theoretic and Uncertainty-Aware Collaboration for Complex Visual Reasoning

    Jusheng Zhang, Yijia Fan, Wenjun Lin, Ruiqi Chen, Haoyi Jiang, Wenhao Chai, Jian Wang, Keze Wang

    NeurIPS 2025

    [Paper] [arXiv] [Code]

  • SOLAR for Offline MARL: Plateau-Triggered Potential Shaping under World-Model Uncertainty

    For sparse-reward offline multi-agent RL, SOLAR uses a learned world model to detect learning plateaus, then adds uncertainty-throttled potential-based reward shaping that preserves the task optimum.

  • Great Minds Think Alike: Contextual Tacit Communication for Decentralized LLM-Agent Cooperation

    [Paper] [Code]

    Coordinates decentralized embodied agents without explicit runtime messaging by retrieving contextual cooperation rules from a Tacit Rule Memory. The rules align local plans with team-level objectives and help agents avoid conflicting actions.

  • Vocabulary Scaling Law: Tuning Open-vocabulary Predictors for Their Openness

    CVPR 2026

    [Paper] [Code]

    CVPR 2026 Highlight

    Shows that an open-vocabulary classifier's robustness to new and distractor classes is bounded by its full-vocabulary performance, and fine-tunes on a submodularly chosen class subset.

    CVPR 2026

  • A Causal Marriage between VLM and IRM from Understanding to Reasoning

  • The 1st LoViF Challenge on Efficient VLM for Multimodal Ad Creative Quality Scoring: Methods and Results

    Jusheng Zhang, Qinhan Lyu, Sizhuo Ma, Rick Cao, Jian Wang, et al.

    CVPR 2026 Workshop Oral

    The report reviews the 1st LoViF challenge at CVPR 2026, in which participants predicted Amazon product ratings from images and text with lightweight VLMs, and benchmarks the accuracy-efficiency trade-off (Pearson correlation versus parameters, FLOPs and latency) across the top-performing solutions.

  • Why Keep Your Doubts to Yourself? Trading Visual Uncertainties in Multi-Agent Bandit Systems

    [Paper] [arXiv] [Code]

  • CoMem: Compositional Concept-Graph Memory for Vision–Language Adaptation

    H. Zhou, J. Tang, Jusheng Zhang, Y. Li, C. Xiao, L. Hou, Z. Ke, J. Yao

    ICLR 2026

    [Paper] [Code]

    Stores continual vision-language knowledge as a compact graph of concepts and relations, rehearsing in feature space from sampled subgraphs instead of raw images to limit forgetting.

  • Markets of Minds: A 51-Billion-State Economy of Intelligence

    Jusheng Zhang, Xuekun Wu, Wenhao Wang, Mark Wang, Ming-Hsuan Yang

    arXiv 2026 (Preprint)

    [Paper] [Code]

    Treats expert-model assistance across a population of agents as a constrained allocation problem, scoring each agent-service pair by expected gain minus regression risk before any expert output is observed.

  • RaCoT: Plug-and-play Contrastive Example Generation Mechanism for Enhanced LLM Reasoning Reliability

    K. Cai†, Jusheng Zhang†, Y. Fan, J. Yang, K. Wang

    AAAI 2026

    [Paper] [arXiv] [Code]

    AAAI 2026 Oral

    Before retrieval, generates a similar question with a different answer and distills their differences into a prompt that steers the LLM toward answer-determining details, improving RAG robustness.

  • LLM-CAS: Dynamic Neuron Perturbation for Real-Time Hallucination Correction

    Jusheng Zhang, N. Liu, Y. Fan, et al.

    AAAI 2026

    [Paper] [arXiv] [Code]

    Treats real-time hallucination correction as hierarchical reinforcement learning, where an agent temporarily perturbs selected neurons during inference without permanently changing model weights.

  • Top-Down Semantic Refinement for Image Captioning

    Jusheng Zhang, K. Cai, J. Yang, et al.

    AAAI 2026

    [Paper] [arXiv] [Code]

    Treats image captioning as top-down planning and uses an efficient Monte Carlo tree search to refine captions from global structure to fine detail, reducing hallucination.

  • MAT-Agent: Adaptive Multi-Agent Training Optimization

    Jusheng Zhang, K. Cai, Y. Fan, K. Wang

    NeurIPS 2025

    [Paper] [arXiv] [Code]

    Four bandit-driven agents adjust augmentation, optimizer, learning-rate schedule and loss during multi-label image classification training, guided by a composite reward for accuracy, rare classes and stability.

  • Tri-MARF: Tri-Modal Multi-Agent Framework for 3D Object Annotation

    Jusheng Zhang, Y. Fan, Z. Wen, et al.

    NeurIPS 2025

    [Paper] [arXiv] [Code]

    Three agents annotate 3D objects: a vision-language agent captions multi-view renders, an aggregation agent selects the best candidates, and a gating agent checks them against point-cloud geometry.

  • DrDiff: Dynamic Routing Diffusion with Hierarchical Attention

    Jusheng Zhang, Yijia Fan, Kaitong Cai, Zimeng Huang, Xiaofei Sun, Jian Wang, Chengpei Tang, Keze Wang

    EMNLP 2025

    [Paper] [arXiv] [Code]

    EMNLP 2025 Oral

    A diffusion framework for long-text generation that routes computation by text complexity, uses hierarchical sparse attention with linear cost, and cuts sampling steps via semantic anchor states.

  • OSC: Cognitive Orchestration through Dynamic Knowledge Alignment

    Jusheng Zhang, Y. Fan, K. Cai, et al.

    EMNLP 2025 (Findings)

    [Paper] [arXiv] [Code]

    OSC gives each LLM agent a Collaborator Knowledge Model of its teammates' cognitive state, so agents adapt their focus and level of detail to close knowledge gaps.

  • Scaling Laws for Deepfake Detection

    Wenhao Wang, Jusheng Zhang, Longqi Cai, Taihong Xiao, Yuxiao Wang, Ming-Hsuan Yang

    arXiv 2025 (Preprint)

    [Paper] [Code]

    Builds ScaleDF, over 14 million real and fake images spanning 51 domains and 102 generators, and shows deepfake detection error follows power laws as domains and methods scale.
