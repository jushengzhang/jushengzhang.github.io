Jusheng Zhang

I am a Visiting Researcher at Stanford University, working with Joseph C. Wu and Xuekun Wu on agentic AI for life sciences, and a student researcher at Google DeepMind, working with Ming-Hsuan Yang and Liangzhe Yuan on efficient video and vision-language models.

Previously, I was a research intern at Nanyang Technological University (with Kwok-Yan Lam and Yongsen Zheng), Meta AI (Llama 4 team), and Snap Research (with Jian Wang), and a researcher at the HCP Lab of Sun Yat-sen University (with Keze Wang and Liang Lin).

My goal is to help build the next generation of AI agents for scientific discovery.
