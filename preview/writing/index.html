Jusheng Zhang

I am a Visiting Researcher at Stanford University, working with Joseph C. Wu and Xuekun Wu, and a Student Researcher at Google DeepMind, working with Ming-Hsuan Yang and Liangzhe Yuan.

I study how agents can learn from failures, coordinate expertise, and use visual evidence to make better decisions.

Previously, I worked with teams at Nanyang Technological University, Meta AI, Snap Research, and the HCP Lab of Sun Yat-sen University. More about me ↗

My goal is to help build the next generation of AI agents for scientific discovery.
